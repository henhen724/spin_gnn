DEFECT IN MY OWN REGISTRATION, CAUGHT BY ITS OWN D2
========================================================================
The registered estimator was h_j = (n_err - n_err_in_F)/n_at_risk,
described as the DD-unfaithful rate.  IT IS NOT THAT.  `n_err` counts
FIRST ERRORS under the permissive rollout rule and `n_err_in_F` is the
benign subset of those, so the formula counts permissive failures that
also named an unforced cell -- a SUBSET OF FAILURES, not the set of
unfaithful emissions.  A rollout that names an unforced cell and
colours it correctly is not a permissive error at all and never enters
`n_err`; that is the whole content of finding 98.

`n_in_F` is an EMISSION-level count, so the correct estimator is
h_j^ddf = 1 - n_in_F(j)/n_at_risk(j).  Evidence it is emission-level:
median n_in_F/n_at_risk = 0.7432 over 19,889 rows, and n_in_F > n_err
on 19,365 of them, which no error-subset column could do.

CONSEQUENCE, APPLIED BELOW WITHOUT EXCEPTION: G1-G5 as registered do
not test what they claim, and are scored VOID BY SCORER DEFECT rather
than as honest misses.  Everything computed from h^ddf is POST-HOC and
is claimed as nobody's prediction.

  what the registered formula gives, for the record:
  median log S_regd / log S = 1.0000 over 1009 cells --
  i.e. it simply reproduces the permissive solve rate, as it must,
  because its numerator differs from n_err by 2%.

D2 -- MONOTONICITY
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  As registered: S_logic <= S_strict_hat on every cell.  THAT
  INEQUALITY WAS BACKWARDS -- h_logic <= h_perm by construction, so the
  registered product can only be the LARGER of the two.  D2 fired on
  that, which is how the defect above was found.

  AND THE OBVIOUS REPAIR, S_ddf <= S, IS ALSO NOT FORCED.  The two
  criteria are NOT NESTED: S_ddf asks only 'did it ever name an
  unforced cell', while S asks 'did it reach SOLVED with the planted
  colouring'.  A rollout can choose every cell faithfully and still
  fail S -- wrong colour on a forced cell, a grammar slip, early
  termination -- and conversely.  So neither direction is guaranteed,
  and what IS guaranteed is that the TRUE DD-faithful solve rate, the
  intersection of the two, is at most min(S, S_ddf).
  cells with S_ddf > S: 1 of 1106
    r68 rho 0.95: S 0.8575 vs S_ddf 0.8587  (excess +0.0012)
  -> D2 as registered: WRONG INEQUALITY, and its repair is wrong too;
     the quantity that is actually bounded is min(S, S_ddf), reported
     per density below.  The run-level check that matters is that
     rho_50 never moves the easy way, scored next.
