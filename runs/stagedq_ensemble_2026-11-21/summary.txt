Q1b. RECONCILIATION WITH FINDING 10 -- IT NEEDS AMENDING.
    Finding 10 reports ~6x ('at N=60 (d=4,q=3) reaches mean
    DD depth 14.5, which (5,4) does not reach until N~333').
    That was measured WITHOUT the fall constraint. Measured
    here:
      fall=True   1.58x in N, 1.77x in tokens
      fall=False  5.00x in N, 5.48x in tokens
    MECHANISM, read off the rho_min rows below. The fall
    constraint removes the nodes that can never be deduced and
    so must stay hints forever, which is what caps depth near
    the floor -- and it helps the REF ensemble far more than
    the lever, because q=4 d=5 has 0.3827 such nodes against a
    much smaller fraction at q=3 d=4. At N=192 turning fall on
    moves rho_min 0.4729 -> 0.3134 for the REF (-0.1595) but
    only 0.1944 -> 0.1437 for the lever (-0.0507): the REF gains
    3.1x more room below its floor, and that is the gap closing.
    THE 6x DOES NOT TRANSFER TO THE ENSEMBLE WE TRAIN ON.
    (Note: a 'never deducible' COUNT at rho_min is identically
    zero by construction and is not evidence either way --
    greedy removal only drops a hint if DD still recovers
    everything, so the survivors are all deducible. The rho_min
    shift is the measurement; a frac_inf column would be noise.)
