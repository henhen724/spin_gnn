BOTTOM LINE: tying is penalised more by strict scoring, but not by

WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH.  The mode split is POST-HOC and rests on
12 runs, 2 of them in the SOLVED-IT mode per group; Fisher gives p = 0.236
for the 2-of-9 against 2-of-3 rate, which is no evidence of a rate
difference at all.  The conditional gap is a median over two runs each.
It is a hypothesis for a seeded sweep, not a measurement.  p_logic is a
teacher-forced quantity and solve rate is free generation; T5 is the
reminder that improving the former cannot move the latter here.  And
nothing was retrained -- this is the same 1,120-cell table as stage
LOGIC, read two ways.
