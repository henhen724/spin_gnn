WHAT R < 1 MEANS, AND WHAT THIS STAGE DOES NOT ESTABLISH.  R is the
ratio of the free-generation fatal hazard per rollout to the
teacher-forced logical node-error count per rollout.  R ~ 0.5 says
free generation makes about half as many fatal node errors as teacher
forcing makes node errors -- teacher forcing is the HARDER regime
here.  Why is NOT established by this stage.  Candidates, none tested:
  (i) under teacher forcing the model is carried along the reference
      prefix and scored at all n node positions whether or not its own
      policy would have reached them;
  (ii) in free generation it emits cells in an order of its own
      choosing, and stage POS already found the per-step hazard FALLS
      with j (P1, falsified at 31.1% positive), so its later choices
      are its safer ones;
  (iii) R rises monotonically with difficulty, 0.443 at rho 0.75 to
      0.607 at rho 0.50, so whatever it is weakens as n grows.
Separating these needs a measurement this stage does not contain.

median run's colour error rate is exactly 0 at all ten densities and
