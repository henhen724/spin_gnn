BOTTOM LINE: C < 1 is an artefact of extrapolating a straight line in
log p from p ~ 0.995 out to p = 1.  Fit only the 8 runs nearest p = 1
and the prefactor returns to 0.997 at rho 0.50, where the pooled fit
said 0.797.  The S = p^(0.93 n) law does NOT need a second failure
mode: at p = 1 the models solve everything, as a fatal-logical-error
account requires.  The pooled C is biased low because log S is convex
in log p and the bulk of the 79 runs sit far from p = 1.
