WHAT THIS DOES TO STAGE RULE2'S VERDICT
==============================================================================
RULE2 measured h_rule FALLING from 0.00057 at rho = 0.50 to 0.00053 at rho = 0.33 (a factor 0.94x) and refused to read it as the rule improving.
That refusal is now quantified rather than asserted: the censored rollouts would only have to be 1.19x worse at the rule than the survivors for the fall to disappear. Nothing in these shards rules that out, because a censored rollout is never scored again after its first error.
So RULE2's 'unanswerable' stands, and the reason is measured: 0.7% of rollouts survive to the last scored index at rho = 0.33, and censoring removed at least 111524 forced emissions, a further 57% on top of the 194407 that were scored.

WHERE THE REGISTRATION WAS WRONG. E2 predicted L > F_obs at rho = 0.33 and the measurement is L/F_obs = 0.57, so censoring removed rather less than it scored, not more. The prediction is a MISS and stays one. It does not rescue the bin, because E4 does not need L to exceed F_obs: h_rule is so small that a lost denominator of 57% is already enough to make the fall vanish at lambda = 1.19.

WHAT IS STILL NOT MEASURED. Which lambda is real. No value of lambda is a measurement and lambda* is never quoted as the frailty that occurred -- only as the frailty that would be required. Settling it needs a rollout that is repaired at its first error and continued, which is generation on existing checkpoints and is hshunt's call, not an action taken here.

SCOPE. This measures the INSTRUMENT, not an architecture. Nothing here ranks the 31 cells, and the h_rule ranking remains density-dependent (finding 122, Spearman +0.8464) and must not be quoted. The separation from the 0.01 bar is unaffected: every number above is a statement about which emissions got scored, not about which model is smaller.

Earlier decks on this project call n_err 'first errors of any kind', which is loose: a rollout that names an unforced cell and gets the colour RIGHT is a stopping-condition failure but is NOT censored. The correction is stated in this stage's deck.
The competence degrades with density -- 71% right at rho = 0.95 down to 50% at rho = 0.33 -- so the rollouts censoring removes are not incompetent.
