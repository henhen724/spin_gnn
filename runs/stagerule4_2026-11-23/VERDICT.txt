WHAT THIS SAYS, AND WHAT IT STILL DOES NOT

  On the one slice where censoring is exactly zero, h_rule(0) is
  0.002038 pooled over rho >= 0.80, 0.002626 at rho = 0.50 and 0.002184
  pooled over the four bins below rho_c = 0.465.

  E1 HIT and E2 MISSED under a passing E5. The pooled FALL
  does not survive the clean slice -- so stage RULE2's
  reading was right to refuse it -- but the clean slice does
  not show the registered 1.3x degradation either.

  E2 AND E3 BOTH MISSED FOR THE SAME REASON, AND IT IS NOT
  NOISE. Both tested a MONOTONE trend in rho. h_rule(0) is
  not monotone: it is humped, peaking well above rho_c, and
  a ratio of the four lowest bins against the four highest
  straddles the peak and nets out near 1. The registration
  chose the wrong shape, not the wrong size -- see the
  post-hoc arms section, where both sides are monotone to
  |Spearman| > 0.97 and both ratios exclude 1.

  STILL NOT MEASURED, and j = 0 does not fix it: h_rule
  conditions on the model NAMING a forced cell. That share runs
  96.3% at rho = 0.95 down to 75.9% at rho = 0.33, so the
  denominators are differently composed across densities. The
  slice is censoring-free, NOT unbiased.

  Also not addressed: all 79 runs are evaluated on the SAME 400
  instances per bin, so errors concentrated on a few hard
  instances would widen the true interval beyond both the
  Poisson and the delete-one-run figures. The shards carry no
  per-instance breakdown, so this is named, not corrected.

  NO TRAINING FOLLOWS FROM ANY OF THIS, as registered.

  THE RATIO INTERVALS ABOVE ASSUME EMISSIONS ARE EXCHANGEABLE,
  AND THE JACKKNIFE SAYS THEY ARE NOT -- deleting one run moves
  the pooled ratio far more than Poisson counting allows, so a
  few runs carry much of the error mass. The SHAPE does not rest
  on that assumption: a monotone ordering across independent
  density bins is a distribution-free statement, tested here by
  shuffling the bin labels.

    high arm   5 bins  Spearman -1.0000   Monte-Carlo p = 8.03e-03
    low arm   10 bins  Spearman +0.9758   Monte-Carlo p = 2.00e-05

  That is the claim this stage actually supports: the SHAPE is
  solid, the MAGNITUDE of any one ratio is not, because the 79
  runs are not 79 exchangeable replicates. Consistent with trap
  3 -- the per-architecture ranking is itself density-dependent.

  So the ORDERING on both sides of the peak is resolved -- in a
  slice with no censoring at all -- while the SIZE of the drop
  is not. The weaker statement is the one that survives both
  intervals, and it is still enough: h_rule(0) does not keep
  falling monotonically with rho, and it does not keep rising
  either. The fall below the peak is therefore NOT purely a
  survivorship artefact -- some of it is real, because at j = 0
  there is no survivorship to blame.

  Stage RULE3's lambda* = 1.19 bounded how much censoring COULD
  explain; it never claimed censoring DID explain it. A fall
  surviving at zero censoring points at lambda near 1, the same
  direction finding 124's off-support competence pointed.

  The first deduction is where the rule errors are: h_rule(0) is
  3x to 7x h_rule(j>=1) at every one of the fourteen densities.
  So pooled h_rule is a LENGTH-WEIGHTED average of a bad first
  index and a clean tail, and rho sets the length (mean_n_blank
  runs 3 to 32). The weight w0 is where that bites.

  BUT w0 DOES NOT EXPLAIN THE FALL. Split at the peak:

    high arm  rho 0.95 -> 0.75 :  w0 0.338 -> 0.109 (0.32x)
              h_rule(0) 0.001150 -> 0.003203 (2.79x)
              pooled    0.000622 -> 0.000846 (1.36x)
    low arm   rho 0.75 -> 0.33 :  w0 0.109 -> 0.123 (1.13x)
              h_rule(0) 0.003203 -> 0.002084 (0.65x)
              pooled    0.000846 -> 0.000530 (0.63x)

  On the LOW arm w0 barely moves (0.109 -> 0.123), so there is no
  dilution left to do the work, and the pooled fall (1.60x) and
  the censoring-free j = 0 fall (1.54x) agree. THE FALL BELOW THE
  PEAK IS REAL -- not dilution, and not censoring, because there
  is none at j = 0.

  What dilution DOES explain is the opposite: on the HIGH arm w0
  falls 3.09x, which is why h_rule(0) rises 2.79x while the
  pooled rate rises only 1.36x. Pooling MASKED a rise; it did not
  manufacture a fall.

  This does not contradict stage RULE3. lambda* = 1.19 was what
  censoring WOULD need to erase the pooled fall, never a claim
  that it does. The fall surviving at zero censoring is direct
  evidence that lambda sits near 1, which is what finding 124's
  off-support competence argued on separate grounds.

  CAVEAT, stated not corrected: h_rule(j>=1) IS censored and
  biased downward, so the j=0-vs-later gap is an upper estimate
  of itself. The weight w0 is not -- it is a count of emissions,
  and the pooled column is exactly stages RULE2 and RULE3's
  number (D3 passed at 0.000e+00).
