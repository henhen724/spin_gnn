## 0. Provenance and disclosure — read this before the predictions

**The hypothesis was formed by looking at data.** Stage ABLATE (finding 106)
probed 43 of the 79 runs in `runs/stagebound_2026-11-17/bound_runs.csv`. Its
seven registered predictions (A1–A7) were about the *ranked* attention heads.
The result below was **not registered in ABLATE** — it came out of the
exploratory ladder, post hoc. Everything in §3 is therefore a prediction about
runs that have **never been probed**, generated by a pattern seen in runs that
have.

**The post-hoc observation.** Blocking the query's attention to the hint-colour
tokens of the queried cell's neighbours (condition `ALLHINT`, "hop 2") destroys
below-gate runs and leaves gate-passing runs untouched:

| condition | what is blocked | median acc, 19 gate-passing | median acc, 24 below-gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `INTACT` | nothing | 1.000 | 1.000 |
| `ALLPAIRS` | the queried cell's own `BEGIN_GRAPH` row (hop 1) | 1.000 | 0.980 |
| **`ALLHINT`** | **neighbours' hint COLOUR tokens (hop 2)** | **1.000** | **0.235** |
| `ALLBOTH` | both of the above | 0.695 | 0.225 |
| `ALLROWS6` | all six graph rows naming the cell | 0.350 | 0.355 |
| `ALLADJ` | those six rows plus the hints | 0.215 | 0.215 |
| `ALLSEC` | the whole hints + graph sections (positive control) | 0.055 | 0.060 |

All at ρ = 0.75, 200 held-out instances per run, query at deduction index j = 0.
`ALLHINT` is the **only** rung that separates the two groups.

**Specificity on the seen 43.** Spearman correlation between `acc` and per-run
ρ₅₀ (`rho50_pub` from `bound_runs.csv`; lower ρ₅₀ = better model, so a negative
correlation means "survives the ablation ⇒ better model"):

| condition | all 43 | within `full` (n=13) | within `no_mlp` (n=21) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`ALLHINT`** | **−0.764** | **−0.664** | **−0.824** |
| `ALLROWS6` | +0.366 | +0.165 | +0.025 |
| `ALLPAIRS` | −0.183 | +0.061 | −0.410 |
| `ALLSEC` | +0.097 | +0.420 | −0.299 |

`ALLHINT` is the only condition whose relationship survives conditioning on
architecture family. This is the opposite of how the stage HEADS `H_self` lead
died (Spearman +0.000 within `full`, −0.107 within `no_mlp`, +0.326 only
pooled — i.e. it ordered families, not competence). `H_self` is recorded as a
dead lead and is not revisited here.

**Classifier on the seen 43.** `acc(ALLHINT) ≥ 0.9` predicts `ρ₅₀ < 0.740`
correctly on 40 of 43 (0.930); base rate 19 of 43 pass.

**Known counterexample, disclosed in advance.** `r18` = `L=6 h=4 full ff256`
scores `ALLHINT` = 1.000 while sitting *above* the gate. Surviving the ablation
is therefore **not sufficient** for passing the gate, and no prediction below
claims it is.

**The width and depth confounds are already dead.** At `no_mlp` d=256 with
width held exactly fixed, gate-passing `L=12` scores 0.985 while below-gate
`L=4`/`L=6`/`L=6 ep100`/`L=8` score 0.235/0.235/0.215/0.200. Restricting to
depth ≥ 12 instead, gate-passing median is 1.000 against below-gate 0.235.
Neither width nor depth explains the split in the slice where the other is
held fixed.

## 1. The claim under test

> A model that still deduces when its direct access to the neighbours' hint
> colours is removed has learned something that reconstructs those colours —
> and that capacity, not the architecture it lives in, is what predicts
> whether the model solves sudoku instances at low hint density.

The rival account, which the seen data **cannot** distinguish from it:

> `acc(ALLHINT)` is a property of the architecture family. `full` models
> happen to score high and happen to pass the gate; `tied_no_mlp` models
> happen to score low and happen to fail. Nothing causal, just two
> consequences of the same design choice.

The seen 43 cannot separate these because **family and competence are
perfectly confounded in them for the decisive family**: all five probed
`tied_no_mlp` runs have ρ₅₀ ∈ [0.752, 0.856] — every one above the gate — and
`ALLHINT` ∈ [0.115, 0.190], the lowest family median of all (0.160).

## 3. Predictions

Measurement throughout: `acc` = fraction of 200 held-out instances on which the
model's argmax colour at the j = 0 query token is the correct one, at hint
density ρ = 0.75, with the named attention pairs masked to −inf. Conditions
run: `INTACT`, `ALLHINT`, `ALLPAIRS`, `ALLROWS6`, `ALLSEC`. Densities
0.90 / 0.75 / 0.60; ρ = 0.75 is the registered one, the others are reported.

**B1 — out-of-sample separation.** Median `acc(ALLHINT)` over the 4
gate-passing held-out runs ≥ **0.80**; median over the other 32 ≤ **0.40**.
*Falsifier:* gate-passing median ≤ 0.40.
*Support warning, in advance:* a median of n = 4 is weak evidence on its own;
B1b is the count form and is the one to read if they disagree.

**B1b — count form.** At least **3 of the 4** gate-passing held-out runs score
`acc(ALLHINT) ≥ 0.70`, and at most **8 of the 32** others do.
*Grounding:* on the seen 43, 18 of 19 runs with ρ₅₀ < 0.740 cleared 0.70
(94.7%), against 3 of 24 above the gate (12.5%, which would be ~4 of 32).
*Falsifier:* ≤ 1 of 4, or ≥ 16 of 32.

**B2 — the within-cell dissociation. This is the stage's point.**
Both `r76` and `r74` score `acc(ALLHINT) ≥ 0.80`, while their same-cell seed
siblings `r77` and `r73` both score ≤ **0.40**.
*Competence account predicts:* ≥ 0.80 for r76, r74.
*Family account predicts:* ≈ 0.16 for all four, like r75 (0.115) and r72 (0.170).
*Falsifier:* both r76 and r74 ≤ 0.40 — the family account wins outright and the
whole `ALLHINT` lead is demoted to an architecture marker.
*SPLIT if:* exactly one of r76, r74 clears 0.80, or the siblings do not stay low.

**B3 — specificity, within family.** Spearman(`acc(ALLHINT)`, ρ₅₀) over the 19
held-out `no_mlp` runs (ρ₅₀ range 0.644–0.777) ≤ **−0.40**.
*Grounding:* −0.824 on the 21 seen `no_mlp` runs, whose ρ₅₀ range is wider.
The bar is set well inside that because the held-out range is compressed.
*Falsifier:* ≥ 0.

**B4 — the control condition stays flat, relatively.**
|Spearman(`ALLROWS6`, ρ₅₀)| over those same 19 runs is at least **0.30**
smaller in magnitude than |Spearman(`ALLHINT`, ρ₅₀)| over the same 19.
*Registered as a relative comparison, not an absolute bar,* because the seen
`ALLROWS6` values (+0.366 pooled, +0.025 within `no_mlp`) sit too close to any
absolute bar I would pick, and picking one from a number I have already seen is
the stage LOCAL mistake.
*Falsifier:* `ALLROWS6` correlates at least as strongly as `ALLHINT`.

**B5 — replication of the hop asymmetry (low information, stated as such).**
Over all 36: median `acc(ALLPAIRS)` − median `acc(ALLHINT)` ≥ **0.40**.
*Grounding:* 0.745 among the seen below-gate runs, and the held-out set is 32/36
non-gate-passing. This is a replication check, not a discovery; it is
registered so that a failure would invalidate the instrument.
*Falsifier:* ≤ 0.

**B6 — the positive control is competence-blind.** Median `acc(ALLSEC)` over
the 36 ≤ **0.15**, and |median(4 gate-passing) − median(32 others)| ≤ **0.20**.
*Rationale:* blocking the whole hints + graph sections should break everything,
including the models that survive `ALLHINT`. If `ALLSEC` also separated the
groups, `ALLHINT` would be measuring general robustness rather than hop 2.
*Falsifier:* median > 0.30, or a separation > 0.40.

## 4. Declared unscoreable in advance

- **Pooled Spearman over all 36 runs.** Reported, never scored. The held-out
  ρ₅₀ IQR is 0.018; 32 of 36 runs lie in [0.742, 0.777]. Any pooled rank
  correlation here is an ordering of negligible differences (stage PROPER R3:
  "range and correlation must be read together").
- **`full` (n=7), `linear` (n=3), `rf_mlp` (n=1).** Reported descriptively.
- **Any claim that surviving `ALLHINT` is *sufficient* for passing the gate.**
  `r18` already refutes it (§0).
- **Classifier accuracy on the held-out 36.** Base rate 32/36 = 0.889.

## 5. E-checks — all four must pass or the stage is VOID

- **E1** `acc(INTACT)` = 1.0000 on ≥ 34 of the 36 runs at ρ = 0.75.
- **E2** every ablated condition removes > 0 attention mass on every
  (run, ρ) pair; `mass_removed` recorded per row.
- **E3** locality: the pairs blocked by `ALLHINT` are exactly the hint-colour
  tokens of the queried cell's neighbours — 0 violations, checked by
  recomputing the blocked set from the instance's adjacency.
- **E4** completeness: `n_q = 200` on every row, 36 runs × 3 densities ×
  5 conditions = 540 rows, no condition missing for any run.

## 6. Scope

Trains nothing. Inference only, on 36 existing checkpoints opened read-only.
Touches no closed knob (`role count` stays closed; no k-sweep is implied —
r74/r76 differ from r72/r75 by **seed**, not by k). Orthogonal to the four
registered questions waiting on hshunt; none of them is answered, assumed or
pre-empted here. CPU-only, ~1 hour wall clock across 8 array tasks.
