    A DEFECT IN MY OWN REGISTRATION, and it is the second half of O5.
    REGISTERED.md argues in prose that benign censoring SHRINKS the
    denominator and therefore RAISES h_j -- which is right -- and then
    predicts that UNDOING the censoring raises r_j, which is the
    opposite direction: undoing it ENLARGES the denominator and must
    LOWER h_j.  The two sentences contradict each other.  The
    prediction is scored exactly as written, so this half is a MISS if
    the correction lowers r_j, and the prose was the correct half.
    The correction is an UPPER bound on the denominator (a benignly
    censored rollout is credited as still at risk at every later j), so
    the corrected r_j is a LOWER bound.

POST-HOC, NOT REGISTERED -- WHAT R ACTUALLY IS.
    O1 says r_0 = 0.499 where the two instruments are provably on the
    same prefix, the same instances and the same greedy argmax over the
    same 58 symbols (rollout_batch takes an unconstrained argmax, so at
    j = 0 the emitted token is the SAME TOKEN in both regimes).  Two
    instruments cannot disagree about one token unless they are scoring
    different EVENTS, and they are:

      e_j  counts  `the cell named was not forced by DD'
      h_j  counts  `the pair emitted was not an ok pair'

    and stagelogic_score.py's walk_continuation defines an ok pair as
    node_valid AND color_correct, where node_valid is only `not already
    coloured' -- NOT `forced'.  So naming a cell DD has not yet forced is
    not an error in free generation at all, provided the colour is right.
    Confirmed on the first-error records: of 20657 first errors at j = 0
    over the six scored densities, 20656 are kind=color_wrong and 1 is
    node_invalid; err_uncolored is 1 on all but that one.  The cell
    CHOICE is never itself the error.

    READ, and it answers 97's open question:  the model has LEARNED
    direct deduction -- on a cell DD really has forced it errs 0.1-0.3%
    of the time.  What it has not learned is WHEN TO WAIT: it names a
    not-yet-forced cell 3.7% of the time at rho 0.95 rising to 25.3% at
    rho 0.50, and then colours it correctly about HALF the time, against
    1/q = 0.25 for a blind guess.  Teacher forcing counts every one of
    those premature picks as a logical error; free generation only
    counts the mis-coloured half.  R ~ 0.5 IS THAT HALF.  Teacher
    forcing is therefore not the harder regime -- it is the stricter
    SCORE, and R is the rate at which a premature guess happens to be
    right.  This also explains O1 and O2 together: the one-half is a
    property of a single decision, which is why it is already there at
    j = 0 and why no shape in j was ever needed to produce it.

    What this does NOT establish: the colour accuracy on unforced cells
    is ~0.5 at every density, but WHY it is 0.5 rather than 0.25 or 0.9
    is not measured here.  A cell DD has not forced may still be heavily
    constrained (q - 1 colours excluded of q), so 0.5 is not evidence of
    guessing at random, and the n_F_sum column needed to size that is
    not used by this stage.
