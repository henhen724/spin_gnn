WHAT THIS ANSWERS, AND WHAT IT LEAVES OPEN

  One hit out of five, and the hit was disclosed in advance as a
  consistency check.  On the registration's own scoring this stage
  mostly missed.  But four of those five predictions missed by being
  TOO WEAK, not by being wrong, and the thing they were too weak to
  state is the largest result in this line:

  THE COLOUR STEP IS AN EXACT CANDIDATE-SET COMPUTATION, IN 77 OF 79
  RUNS, WITH NO EXCEPTIONS AT ALL.

  Not 'almost always', which is what R1 and R2 were built to measure.
  Zero improper draws in 40449 wrong unforced emissions across all
  fourteen densities.  When these models write a colour, it is one the
  constraints they can already see still allow -- even when the colour
  is wrong, even on the hardest puzzles, even in the runs that solve
  almost nothing.  The 147 counterexamples in the whole dataset come
  from 2 runs and 2 architectures, and 144 of them from a single run of
  the smallest architecture tried.

  Set against 102, that gives the sharpest statement this project has
  of what these models actually do:

    THE CELL STEP IS NOT DEDUCTIVE  -- its apparent preference for
    near-forced cells is selection on k, the number of coloured
    neighbours, and dies under the within-instance control (102).

    THE COLOUR STEP IS DEDUCTIVE    -- given whatever cell was named,
    the colour respects every visible constraint, exactly (103).

  A constraint-respecting colour rule bolted to a non-deductive cell
  rule.  That is a much more specific object than 'the model does
  direct deduction' or 'it does not', and it is the first positive
  evidence in this line that ANYTHING in the model represents the
  candidate set.  It also says where the solve rate is lost: not in
  writing illegal colours, but in choosing cells whose candidate set
  has more than one member and then picking from it.

  WHAT IT DOES NOT SHOW.  Exactness of the colour step does not mean
  the model COMPUTES the candidate set and samples from it; the same
  record is produced by any mechanism that never emits a seen colour,
  including simple inhibition from the neighbour tokens in context.
  Distinguishing those needs the model's internals, not its output,
  and this stage reads output only.

  WHAT IS STILL BLOCKED.  Everything about the CELL step still waits on
  a generator change -- larger q, or instances where high-k cells keep
  three or more candidates -- because c and k are near-collinear on the
  ensemble that exists.  That needs a job, and therefore hshunt.  This
  stage did not need one: it was a column that had been sitting in
  B_hazard unread since stage POS.
