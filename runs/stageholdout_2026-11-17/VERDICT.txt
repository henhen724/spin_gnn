D3 — MONOTONICITY AT THE NEW DENSITIES, AND WHY IT MISSES
  S_ddf > S on 1 of 1106 cells (0 of them below the fit grid)
  registered: 0 — 'any violation is a scorer bug'  ->  MISS
    L=16 h=4 tied_no_mlp d192  run r68  rho 0.95:  S 0.857500  S_ddf 0.858740  excess +0.001240
  THE PREDICTION WAS ILL-FOUNDED, AND THE VIOLATION IS NOT A BUG.
  S_ddf <= S is not guaranteed by anything.  S_perm = S exactly at
  that cell, so this is not product-versus-measurement slack: the
  two criteria are simply NOT NESTED.  The permissive hazard counts
  rollouts that got a colour wrong; the DD-faithful hazard counts
  rollouts that named a cell direct deduction had not yet forced.
  A rollout can name an unforced cell and still guess the right
  colour — penalised by h_ddf, not by h — and can get a colour
  wrong on a cell that WAS forced — penalised by h, not by h_ddf.
  Neither set contains the other, so neither solve rate dominates.
  The inequality that does hold by construction is the one stage
  BOUND registered, S_joint <= min(S_ddf, S_perm), because the
  joint criterion is the UNION of the two failure sets:
    S_ddf > S_perm on 1 of 1106 cells — the same
    cell, and the excess is one wrong colour on a forced cell.
  CONSEQUENCE: stage GATE's rho_50_ddf >= rho_50_published is an
  EMPIRICAL fact on 1105 of 1106 cells, not a logical one, and
  should stop being described as if the criterion were strictly
  stronger.  It is stronger in one direction only.
