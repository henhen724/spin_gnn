## Findings (notebooks committed; detail in LOG)

1. **There is no sharp critical density** (`notebooks/sudoku_finite_size_2026-10-24`). The old 0.99→0.44 curve is the *transformer* degrading against a DD baseline pinned at 1.0. `random`-hint solvability runs off to rho=1 (midpoint 0.9296→0.9875 for N=24→384); T ~ N^a with a = +0.18 (rho=0.95) → +0.38 (rho=0.50), never 0. `rho_min` is where greedy removal stops, not a critical point. Holds for every (degree, n_colors) — finding 10.
2. **The model is not running DD** — probes, algorithm-ID, counterfactual, SAE all agree.
3. **The frontier probe measures width, not competence**: peak AUROC Spearman **+0.25** with solve_rate, **+0.85** with `d_model`.
4. **`val_nll` barely predicts solve_rate across models, r = +0.33** (34 runs). "val_nll fell" is not evidence. (Within one model it does predict it — finding 14.)
5. **Depth costs something, independent of density** (`48863464`): at rho=0.75 realized density is identical across bins yet solve_rate falls 0.980→0.931 over T=1→4.
6. **Training-set size was the binding constraint** — pinned at 5000 for 31 runs; still the strongest lever at N=24 and **not saturated at 80000**. Does **not** extend to N=96 — finding 15.
7. **The depth cost is NOT in the token-level slots** (`48868696`). Refutes the chaining-colors guess.
8. **What tracks competence is `next_node`** — peak accuracy Spearman **+0.96** with solve_rate, peaking at the **last** layer unlike the frontier.
9. **The easy-end collapse was an OOD artifact, now closed** (`49430217`/`49434190`, 4/4 pre-registrations held). `rho_max=0.95` at N=24 is 22 hints, so k=0,1 are outside training support. Best model scores **exactly 1.000, 0/300 failures, at every in-distribution hint count** and breaks only above the ceiling; **280 of 282** 24-hint failures are `empty BEGIN_ROUND`, the predicted signature. Caveat: `d0384 x 5000` declines in-distribution too, so this is not an ensemble-wide guarantee.
10. **(degree, n_colors) buys depth ~6x cheaper than raising N** (`49509101`, all 21 cells; `notebooks/sudoku_control_param_2026-10-25`, sent). `rho_min` falls monotonically with the propagation ratio `d/(q-1)`, 0.836 at ratio 1.00 → 0.011 at 5.00. At N=60, (d=4,q=3) reaches mean DD depth **14.5**, which (5,4) does not reach until **N≈333**. Depth has an **interior optimum** in the ratio: `d=q-1` is degenerate (T≡1.0), which is why prediction (b) failed backwards. **The matched-density caveat in the 10-24 version was wrong** (drawn from 12 cells, false on 21). The panels *invert* the colour count's role: at each cell's own floor more colours is shallower and depth peaks at ratio 2.0–2.7; at matched rho=0.55 more colours is deeper and depth falls monotonically with ratio inside each q. Mechanism is the floor. Both orderings hold at N=960. **(d=10,q=6) is the caveat-free depth source** — floor 0.507 vs the baseline's 0.468, so 8.4 vs 6.1 at N=60 and 28.0 vs 14.8 at N=960 at essentially matched density.
11. **Network depth does NOT track reasoning depth — L\*(T) is flat at 3 at N=24** (`49433454`, deck sent). L\*, the smallest `num_layers` reaching 0.90 on depth-T instances at fixed density, is **3 at every depth** (T=1..3 at rho=0.75, T=2..5 at rho=0.60), **slope +0.00 layers per round** against a pre-registered +3. The informative failure is (a): **L=2 scores 0.686 at T=1**, so the knee is a fixed cost paid before any chaining. A weak form survives in the *margin* (L3→L8 gain monotone over T=2..5) but at Cochran–Armitage **p=0.088** — suggestive only, and flat L\* does not depend on it.
12. **The N=24 recipe transfers to N=48** (`49433453`): 0.9033 vs 0.9837, clearing the 0.70 gate. Depth range widens as intended (rho=0.60 spans T=3..7), and here the density confound points the *wrong* way (`r=+0.893`).
13. **At matched parameters, depth beats MLP width outright — the L=3 knee is serial, not capacity** (`49510197`/`49521537`; `notebooks/sudoku_ff_depth_2026-10-25`, sent). At **1.65M** matched, L=4/ff=256 **0.9587** vs L=2/ff=1024 **0.4727**, p=9.9e-204; at **2.44M**, L=6/ff=256 **0.9767** vs L=3/ff=1024 **0.9500**, 65–25, p=3.0e-05; and L=6/ff=256 at 2.45M **ties L=12/ff=1024 at 9.55M** (+0.0053, p=0.37) — the top of the ladder at **3.9x fewer parameters**. **H1 is dead**: L=2/ff=4096 at 4.80M, 1.97x the knee's budget, scores 0.4920, and it mildly *overfit* (val_nll ended 0.019 above its minimum), so capacity is refuted backwards. Width pathology real at p=4.6e-33.
14. **L\*(T) is flat in T at every size measured, and its level tracks density, not depth** (`49509466` + `49523504`; `notebooks/sudoku_depth_vs_n_2026-10-25`, 8-page deck, sent). The new L=3 cell scores **0.8293 [0.8095, 0.8475]** against L=4's 0.9093, so **L\*(48)=4** on the aggregate — pre-registration (a) confirmed, and the cross-N question is closed in the direction that depth tracks **graph size**. Per bin, L\*(T) is constant in all four fully searched series: 3 at both densities at N=24, 3 at rho=0.75 and 4 at rho=0.60 at N=48. **15 bins, 0 rising.** At rho=0.60 L=3's deficit is a *uniform* offset (0.875/0.846/0.860/0.835/0.827), not concentrated at deep T, so the extra layer is not buying deduction rounds. (b) confirmed: 150-epoch L=12 beats 300-epoch by **+0.0280**, 106–64, **p=0.0016**. (c) confirmed: L=8 vs L=12 is +0.0140, p=0.076, inside the 0.02 band — and **L=8 (0.9533) is the best N=48 cell**, consistent with L=12 overfitting.
15. **At N=96 the recipe hits a ceiling below the threshold, and it is optimization, not data** (`49524030`, same deck). Shared pool **0.8267 [0.8067, 0.8450]**, so pre-registration (a) is **falsified** and the 36 h N=96 layer scan stays unsubmitted. Decisively, rho=0.75 clears 0.90 in all 4 bins (1.000/0.984/0.983/0.947) but rho=0.60 in **none of 6** (0.860/0.822/0.839/0.754/0.794/0.837) — so L\*(96) at rho=0.60 is **undefined, not large**, which is exactly the failure the probe was written to catch before spending 36 h. (b) confirmed (rho=0.60 spans T=4..9). (c) holds marginally at rho=0.75 (0.053 decline) but **fails** at rho=0.60, where the decline goes non-monotone (r=−0.398 vs ≈−0.9 at N=24/48). Diagnosis contradicts the probe's own pre-registered "next move is data": val_nll bottoms at 1.4588 @ep129 of 150, final 1.4621, last-30 span **0.0043**, and train ends **0.0289 below** val — the model never fits its own training set, so there is no generalization gap for data to close. Candidate cause: **no lr scheduler exists anywhere in the codebase**; every run in the project trained at constant lr.
