## Findings (notebooks committed; detail in LOG)

1. **There is no sharp critical density** (`notebooks/sudoku_finite_size_2026-10-24`). The old 0.99→0.44 curve is the *transformer* degrading against a DD baseline pinned at 1.0. `random`-hint solvability runs off to rho=1 (midpoint 0.9296→0.9875 for N=24→384); T ~ N^a with a = +0.18 (rho=0.95) → +0.38 (rho=0.50), never 0. `rho_min` is where greedy removal stops, not a critical point. Holds for every (degree, n_colors) — finding 10.
2. **The model is not running DD** — probes, algorithm-ID, counterfactual, SAE all agree.
3. **The frontier probe measures width, not competence**: peak AUROC Spearman **+0.25** with solve_rate, **+0.85** with `d_model`.
4. **`val_nll` barely predicts solve_rate across models, r = +0.33** (34 runs). "val_nll fell" is not evidence. (Within one model it does predict it — finding 14.)
5. **Depth costs something, independent of density** (`48863464`): at rho=0.75 realized density is identical across bins yet solve_rate falls 0.980→0.931 over T=1→4.
6. **Training-set size was the binding constraint** — pinned at 5000 for 31 runs; still the strongest lever at N=24, **not saturated at 80000**, and now the *largest* lever at N=96 too (finding 15).
7. **The depth cost is NOT in the token-level slots** (`48868696`). Refutes the chaining-colors guess.
8. **What tracks competence is `next_node`** — peak accuracy Spearman **+0.96** with solve_rate, peaking at the **last** layer unlike the frontier.
9. **The easy-end collapse was an OOD artifact, now closed** (`49430217`/`49434190`, 4/4 pre-registrations held). `rho_max=0.95` at N=24 is 22 hints, so k=0,1 are outside training support. Best model scores **exactly 1.000, 0/300 failures, at every in-distribution hint count**; **280 of 282** 24-hint failures are `empty BEGIN_ROUND`, the predicted signature. Caveat: `d0384 x 5000` declines in-distribution too.
10. **(degree, n_colors) buys depth ~6x cheaper than raising N** (`49509101`; `notebooks/sudoku_control_param_2026-10-25`, sent). `rho_min` falls monotonically with `d/(q-1)`, 0.836 at ratio 1.00 → 0.011 at 5.00. At N=60, (d=4,q=3) reaches mean DD depth **14.5**, which (5,4) does not reach until **N≈333**. Depth has an **interior optimum** in the ratio; `d=q-1` is degenerate (T≡1.0). **The matched-density caveat in the 10-24 version was wrong** (drawn from 12 cells, false on 21): at each cell's own floor more colours is shallower and depth peaks at ratio 2.0–2.7; at matched rho=0.55 it reverses. Mechanism is the floor. Both orderings hold at N=960. **(d=10,q=6) is the caveat-free depth source** — floor 0.507 vs 0.468, so 28.0 vs 14.8 at N=960 at matched density.
11. **Network depth does NOT track reasoning depth — L\*(T) is flat in T wherever it is resolvable** (`49433454`, deck sent; extended by 14/16). L\*, the smallest `num_layers` reaching 0.90 on depth-T instances at fixed density, is **3 at every T** at N=24, **slope +0.00 layers per round** against a pre-registered +3. The informative failure: **L=2 scores 0.686 at T=1**, so the knee is a fixed cost paid before any chaining. **20 bins measured across five series, 0 resolvably rising.** A weak form survives in the *margin* but at Cochran–Armitage **p=0.088**.
12. **The N=24 recipe transfers to N=48** (`49433453`): 0.9033, clearing the 0.70 gate. Depth range widens as intended (rho=0.60 spans T=3..7); the density confound points the *wrong* way (`r=+0.893`).
13. **At matched parameters, depth beats MLP width outright — the knee is serial, not capacity** (`49510197`/`49521537` at N=24, `49587538` at N=48; two decks sent). N=24: at **1.65M**, L=4/ff=256 **0.9587** vs L=2/ff=1024 **0.4727**, p=9.9e-204; L=6/ff=256 at 2.45M **ties L=12/ff=1024 at 9.55M** (+0.0053, p=0.37) — **3.9x fewer parameters**. **Replicated at N=48**, all three matched pairs: +0.5327 / +0.1007 / **+0.0480** (the load-bearing pair *at* the knee, 111–39, p=3.4e-09). Measured slack favours thin by only +0.21%, so margins are 23x–254x the slack. **H1 is dead**: L=2/ff=4096 at 1.97x the knee's budget scores 0.4920 and mildly *overfit*.
14. **L\* in LAYERS is a property of the problem AND the architecture, not the problem alone** (`49509466`/`49523504`, then `49587538`; `notebooks/sudoku_two_ceilings_2026-10-26`, sent). **L\*(24)=3, L\*(48)=4** at ff=1024 — pre-registration (a) confirmed, depth tracks graph size. But narrowing the MLP 4x **moves the N=48 knee to L=6**: L=4/ff=256 scores 0.7113 and clears 0.90 in **none** of the five rho=0.60 bins, while L=6/ff=256 clears all five. **This falsifies the "L\* is architecture-independent" reading of findings 11/14 and every bare "L\*(N)=k" in this file must be read as "at ff=1024".** In *parameters* the thin knee is still the cheaper one (2.51M vs 3.29M, +0.0207, p=0.011), so depth remains the better currency — just not the only one. Also: at rho=0.60 L=3's deficit is a *uniform* offset (0.875/0.846/0.860/0.835/0.827), not concentrated at deep T. 150-epoch L=12 beats 300-epoch by **+0.0280**, p=0.0016.
15. **~~At N=96 the ceiling is optimization, not data~~ — CORRECTED: it was the data budget** (`49524030`, then `49565433`; same deck). The control stalls at 0.8267 and clears 0.90 in **none of 6** rho=0.60 bins, so L\*(96) was **undefined, not large**. I diagnosed that as optimization because train ended **0.0289 below** val — no generalization gap for data to close — and pre-registered that 4x data would gain **less** than cosine lr. **It gained 2.1x more**: cell B (80000) **+0.1007** vs cell A (cosine) **+0.0473**, B over A **+0.0533, 156–76, p=1.6e-07**. The mechanism is in the histories: cell B is the only N=96 run with a *positive* train−val gap (+0.0239 vs −0.0289/−0.0277). **The inference "no overfitting ⇒ more data will not help" is simply wrong — a model can be data-limited while underfitting**, and I should stop using the train−val gap to predict the data lever's size. Cosine is real but smaller than claimed (val min 1.4450 vs 1.4588, below the 0.020 pre-registered).
