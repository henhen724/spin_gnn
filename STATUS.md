## Findings (notebooks committed; detail in LOG)

1. **There is no sharp critical density** (`notebooks/sudoku_finite_size_2026-10-24`, 7-page deck). Corrects the old finding 1, which read as if DD transitions at N=24 — `dd_resolved_frac ≡ 1.0` there and the 0.99→0.44 curve is the *transformer* degrading against a DD baseline pinned at 1.0. Training ensemble (`removal`) is DD-solvable by construction; `random`-hint solvability threshold **runs off to rho=1** (midpoint 0.9296→0.9875 for N=24→384, unhinted nodes at crossing 1.7→4.8). T ~ N^a at **every** density, a = +0.18 (rho=0.95) → +0.38 (rho=0.50), never 0, so rho_min≈0.478 is where greedy removal stops, not a critical point. Now shown to hold for **every** (degree, n_colors), not just (5,4) — see finding 10.
2. **The model is not running DD** — probes, algorithm-ID, counterfactual, SAE all agree.
3. **The frontier probe measures width, not competence** (`48935109`+`48999377`): peak AUROC Spearman **+0.25** with solve_rate, **+0.85** with `d_model`. The new best model *fell* to 0.9067 while solve_rate rose to 0.996.
4. **`val_nll` barely predicts solve_rate across models, r = +0.33** (34 runs). "val_nll fell" is not evidence.
5. **Depth costs something, independent of density** (`48863464`): at rho=0.75, realized density identical across bins yet solve_rate falls 0.980→0.931 over T=1→4; rho=0.60, 0.915→0.798 over T=2→6. Discount rho=0.50 (`r(realized_rho,T) = −0.82`).
6. **Training-set size was the binding constraint** — pinned at 5000 for 31 runs; still the strongest lever and **not saturated at 80000**.
7. **The depth cost is NOT in the token-level slots** (`48868696`). Refutes the chaining-colors guess.
8. **What tracks competence is `next_node`** — peak accuracy Spearman **+0.96** with solve_rate (d0256: 0.789→0.964→0.990). Peaks at the **last** layer, unlike the frontier.
9. **The easy-end collapse was an OOD artifact, now closed** (`49430217`, relabelled by `49434190`, 4/4 pre-registrations held). `removal_path` snapshots at `floor(rho*N)`, so `rho_max=0.95` at N=24 is **22** hints: k=0 (24) and k=1 (23) are outside training support and `mean_o1` averaged two OOD bins with three in-distribution ones. Best model scores **exactly 1.000, 0/300 failures, at every in-distribution hint count** (22/21/20/18/16) and only breaks above the ceiling (0.860 at 23, 0.060 at 24). **280 of the 282** 24-hint failures are `empty BEGIN_ROUND` parse errors — the model opening a round on an instance with nothing to deduce, i.e. exactly the predicted signature, which the first classifier charged to `malformed` because it tested `well_formed` first. **Caveat:** `d0384 x 5000` declines in-distribution too (0.970 at 18, 0.933 at 16) while handling 24 hints fine (0.983) — in-distribution competence is model-dependent, so this is not an ensemble-wide guarantee.

10. **Depth does not only come from raising N — (degree, n_colors) buys it ~8x cheaper** (`49437796`, 12 of 21 cells; `notebooks/sudoku_control_param_2026-10-24`, 7-page deck). `rho_min` falls monotonically with the propagation ratio `d/(q-1)`, 0.836 at ratio 1.00 → 0.011 at 5.00, and **(d=8,q=4) reaches mean DD depth 19.1 near its floor at N=96, which (5,4) does not reach until N≈813.** Two honest caveats: those cells reach that depth at their *own* floor (rho≈0.17 vs 0.48), and **at matched density (5,4) is still the deepest cell on the grid** — the corner buys depth by moving the floor down, not by making deduction harder. Prediction (b) failed *backwards*: the low-ratio cells it expected to be deepest are `d=q-1`, where DD forces nothing and T≡1.0. Depth has an **interior optimum** in the ratio. (a)/(c)/(e) held, (d) failed — `T~N^a` has a>0 at rho=0.90 in every cell that deduces at all (+0.19…+0.25), which is what generalises finding 1.

11. **Network depth does NOT track reasoning depth — L\*(T) is flat at 3** (`49433454`, `notebooks/sudoku_layer_scan_2026-10-24`, 7-page deck, sent). `num_layers` ∈ {1,2,3,4,6,8,12} at N=24, `d_model`/ff pinned. L\*(T), the smallest `num_layers` reaching 0.90 on depth-T instances at fixed density, is **3 at every depth** — T=1..3 at rho=0.75 and T=2..5 at rho=0.60, **slope +0.00 layers per deduction round** against a pre-registered +3. Finding 2's prediction, not the serial one. The informative failure is (a): it predicted one round needs no depth, yet **L=2 scores 0.686 at T=1**, so the L=3 knee is a fixed cost paid *before* any chaining. What *does* track T is the **marginal** value of depth past L\*: the L3→L8 gain at rho=0.60 is +0.011/+0.026/+0.037/+0.051 over T=2,3,4,5 — monotone, so residual error at L\* sits in the deep instances and extra layers remove it preferentially. A weak form of the serial picture survives in the margin; its threshold form is dead. (Measured to L=12 instead the gain is flat and noisy — L=12 is past the knee and overfits; the deck prints both.) **Confound kept, not discounted:** rho=0.60 reported `r(realized_rho,T) = −0.941`, which the sbatch said to discount on, but the total spread is 0.0009 = **0.02 of one hint out of 24**; r tracks only consistency of direction, not size. rho=0.75 has realized_rho *identical* in every bin and carries the claim regardless.

12. **The N=24 recipe transfers to N=48** (`49433453`): `d0256`/L12/`n_train=20000`/300 ep gives `agg_solve_rate` **0.9033** (vs 0.9837 at N=24), clearing the 0.70 falsifier that gated the N=48 layer scan. Depth range widens as intended — rho=0.60 now spans **T=3..7** (vs 2..5 at N=24), solve_rate 0.943→0.894, and here the density confound points the *wrong way* to explain it (`r(realized_rho,T) = +0.893`, deep bins slightly *denser*), so the depth cost survives a conservative confound.
