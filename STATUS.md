## The objective, restated 2026-11-10 — read this before any rung table

**THE GOAL IS A CONCEPTUALLY SIMPLE MODEL THAT MIGHT BE THEORETICALLY ANALYZABLE, EXPLICITLY NOT A MODEL WITH THE FEWEST PARAMETERS.** The old figure of merit — "the standing minimum", tie-broken on smaller trainable count — **was ranking candidates backwards**, and the clearest symptom is that it made a **16-layer** attention-only cell at 2,504,890 the champion over the **6-layer** `full L=6 d256` hub at 4,899,898 that scores the same (0.580 vs 0.592). For anything one would actually analyze, 6 composed layers beats 16 by far more than 2× the parameters costs.

- **What this changes:** trainable count is no longer the figure of merit or the tie-break. Rank by what the OBJECT is — layers composed, operations per layer, weights shared, heads to read — and use `rho_50 ≤ 0.740` as a **gate** ("does it still learn DD"), not as a score.
- **What it does NOT change:** the free line 0.740, `s*` = 0.1484 / 0.2918, the 14-point grid, plateau 1.64, LOO bar 0.0423, 3 seeds with the spread quoted, and the rule that a replacement needs **every** seed to cross. Those are how the measurement is made, not what is optimized; the new objective loosens none of them mid-flight.
- **Consequence already acted on:** stage O's **OB arm (`no_mlp L=20 d160`) was cancelled mid-flight at 12:39** (tasks `51801495_1/_3/_5`). It was 4 layers deeper than anything in the programme and its entire registered upside was being 1.15× smaller — the wrong direction at any count. **OA (tasks 0/2/4) still runs** as the iso-budget reference point.
- **The gap this exposed, and how much of it is now closed.** Findings 27/28's causal audit (350/350 round-0 flips, 0/350 emissions with the forcing broken, 0.980 chaining) was run on **full-architecture N=24** checkpoints and **never on a `no_mlp` one**. **Stage Q closed the round-0 third (finding 58) and stage R the chaining third (finding 59), both 3/3 on the same four arms.** The **guard** third — 0/350 emissions when the forcing is broken, i.e. the model declining to deduce what does not follow — is **still untested on an attention-only cell and has no module in the current tree**; it is the one piece of 27/28 that remains N=24-and-full-only.
