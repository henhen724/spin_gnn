Two separate problems. (i) `train_nll` is `epoch_loss/epoch_n`, averaged over the epoch **while the weights update**, against a `val_nll` on end-of-epoch weights: the bias is **+0.3263 at epoch 10** on the N=96 control and decays as the curve flattens, so the *sign* of a ±0.03 gap carries no information — especially against `n_val=300`, whose own difficulty draw is worth a few hundredths. For **61–67%** of nodes the neighbours cover all q−1 other colours, so the one absent colour block identifies v's own colour.
