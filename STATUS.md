## Findings (notebooks committed; detail in LOG)

1. **There is no sharp critical density** (`notebooks/sudoku_finite_size_2026-10-24`). The old 0.99→0.44 curve is the *transformer* degrading against a DD baseline pinned at 1.0. `random`-hint solvability runs off to rho=1 (midpoint 0.9296→0.9875 for N=24→384); T ~ N^a with a = +0.18 (rho=0.95) → +0.38 (rho=0.50), never 0. `rho_min` is where greedy removal stops, not a critical point. Holds for every (degree, n_colors) — finding 10.
2. **The model is not running DD** — probes, algorithm-ID, counterfactual, SAE all agree.
3. **The frontier probe measures width, not competence**: peak AUROC Spearman **+0.25** with solve_rate, **+0.85** with `d_model`.
4. **`val_nll` barely predicts solve_rate across models, r = +0.33** (34 runs). "val_nll fell" is not evidence.
5. **Depth costs something, independent of density** (`48863464`): at rho=0.75 realized density is identical across bins yet solve_rate falls 0.980→0.931 over T=1→4.
6. **Training-set size was the binding constraint** — pinned at 5000 for 31 runs; still the strongest lever and **not saturated at 80000**.
7. **The depth cost is NOT in the token-level slots** (`48868696`). Refutes the chaining-colors guess.
8. **What tracks competence is `next_node`** — peak accuracy Spearman **+0.96** with solve_rate, peaking at the **last** layer unlike the frontier.
9. **The easy-end collapse was an OOD artifact, now closed** (`49430217`/`49434190`, 4/4 pre-registrations held). `rho_max=0.95` at N=24 is 22 hints, so k=0,1 are outside training support. Best model scores **exactly 1.000, 0/300 failures, at every in-distribution hint count** and breaks only above the ceiling; **280 of 282** 24-hint failures are `empty BEGIN_ROUND`, the predicted signature. Caveat: `d0384 x 5000` declines in-distribution too, so this is not an ensemble-wide guarantee.
10. **(degree, n_colors) buys depth ~6x cheaper than raising N** (`49509101`, all 21 cells; `notebooks/sudoku_control_param_2026-10-25`, 7-page deck, sent). `rho_min` falls monotonically with the propagation ratio `d/(q-1)`, 0.836 at ratio 1.00 → 0.011 at 5.00. At N=60, (d=4,q=3) reaches mean DD depth **14.5**, which (5,4) does not reach until **N≈333**. Depth has an **interior optimum** in the ratio: `d=q-1` is degenerate (DD forces nothing, T≡1.0), which is why prediction (b) failed backwards. **The matched-density caveat in the 10-24 version was wrong** — drawn from 12 cells, false on 21. The two panels *invert* the role of the colour count: at each cell's own floor more colours is shallower (q=6 tops at 9.3 vs q=3's 14.5) and depth peaks at ratio 2.0–2.7; at matched rho=0.55 more colours is deeper (5.0/6.1/6.8/8.4 for q=3,4,5,6) and depth falls monotonically with ratio inside each q. (5,4) is **3rd of 13** at matched density, behind (10,6) and (8,5). Mechanism is the floor: high-q cells have high `rho_min`, so rho=0.55 sits just above theirs. Both orderings hold unchanged at N=960. **(d=10,q=6) is the caveat-free depth source** — floor 0.507 vs the baseline's 0.468, so its gain is measured at essentially the same density: 8.4 vs 6.1 at N=60, 28.0 vs 14.8 at N=960.
11. **Network depth does NOT track reasoning depth — L\*(T) is flat at 3 at N=24** (`49433454`, 7-page deck, sent). L\*, the smallest `num_layers` reaching 0.90 on depth-T instances at fixed density, is **3 at every depth** (T=1..3 at rho=0.75, T=2..5 at rho=0.60), **slope +0.00 layers per round** against a pre-registered +3. The informative failure is (a): **L=2 scores 0.686 at T=1**, so the knee is a fixed cost paid before any chaining. A weak form survives in the *margin* — the L3→L8 gain at rho=0.60 is +0.011/+0.026/+0.037/+0.051 over T=2..5, monotone — but that is 1/92, 5/195, 6/163, 5/98 events, Cochran–Armitage z=+1.70, **p=0.088**: suggestive, not significant, and the flat-L\* result does not depend on it.
12. **The N=24 recipe transfers to N=48** (`49433453`): 0.9033 vs 0.9837, clearing the 0.70 gate. Depth range widens as intended (rho=0.60 spans T=3..7), and here the density confound points the *wrong* way (`r=+0.893`), so the depth cost survives a conservative confound.
13. **At matched parameters, depth beats MLP width outright — the L=3 knee is serial, not capacity** (`49510197` trained, `49521537` scored at n=1500; `notebooks/sudoku_ff_depth_2026-10-25`, 7-page deck, sent). All 3 pre-registrations resolve: at **1.65M** matched, L=4/ff=256 **0.9587** vs L=2/ff=1024 **0.4727**, +0.4860, **p=9.9e-204**; at **2.44M** matched, L=6/ff=256 **0.9767** vs L=3/ff=1024 **0.9500**, +0.0267, 65–25, p=3.0e-05; and L=6/ff=256 at 2.45M **ties L=12/ff=1024 at 9.55M** (+0.0053, p=0.37) — the top of the ladder at **3.9x fewer parameters** (it also ties L=4/ff=1024, 3.23M, at p=1.0). **H1 is dead**: L=2/ff=4096 at 4.80M, **1.97x** the knee's budget, scores 0.4920, and it was not under-trained either — its val_nll turned over at the halfway mark and ended **0.019 above its minimum**, i.e. mild overfitting, so the capacity hypothesis is refuted *backwards*. The width pathology is real at p=4.6e-33.
14. **L\*(T) is flat at N=48 too — at 4, or possibly 3** (`49509466`). L\*(T) = **4 at every depth**, T=3..7 at rho=0.60 and T=2..4 at rho=0.75, with realized-density spread **0.0004 and 0.0000** — no confound to argue about this time. Cells: L=1 0.0033, L=2 0.2433, L=4 0.9033, L=8 0.9533, L=12 0.9533. The flat-in-T half of finding 11 now holds at two sizes over a wider T range; the **cross-N** half is open because the grid {1,2,4,8,12} **skipped L=3**, so L\*(48) ∈ {3,4} (49523504). **Its pre-registered gate failed, upward**: the L=12 cell scored 0.9533 against 49433453's 0.9033, Wilson [0.865, 0.932] — 150 epochs beat 300. Diagnosed, not mysterious: both runs reach the same val minimum (1.2146@ep46, 1.2164@ep36 — training is reproducible) and both then overfit, the 300-epoch run further (+0.1413 vs +0.0497 above its minimum, rising 0.0663 over its second half), and the saved checkpoint is the final epoch. The confound is therefore **cross-job, not within-scan** — all five cells and the whole N=24 arm are 150 ep — so what it invalidates is using 49433453 as a reproducibility anchor, not the L\* comparison.
